INT. THE BACKROOMS
The T suddenly phases through the solid wall, reappearing in a series of rooms. The T looks around, peering behind corners, trying to figure out where he could be. Finally, he attempts to go back through the wall he came out of, only to find it sealed off.
T (panicked): HELLO!? Can you hear me? Dude I can't get back through.
The camera slowly zooms in from afar as the T continues to pound against the wall.
In the distance, a faint mumbling is heard, like someone talking. The T swivels to look toward where he heard it from. He begins to back away.
T (shaky): Haha, I see what's going on. You made a custom map to play a prank on me. Very funny. Now let me out.
The camera zooms further into the abyss. Suddenly, from the dark, a single word, loud and clear:
VOICE: No.
T (still shaken): Dude, did you really think this would work? You know I can just fly out, right?
The T attempts to type "fly" into his console, but it doesn't work. He also sees the poem is already pasted into his console.
T (frantic now): What's that stupid command again? You know what, dude? Next time, how about I host the server?
Suddenly, the mumbling goes silent, and the dark corridor seems to have quieted. Then, from the deep, footsteps.
T (relieved): Mhm. So you had your fun, and now it's time to get back to the video we were making, huh? Well, it was pretty good, I'll admit. I was genuinely freaked out there for a second. But there's no good smoke lineups in here, are there? I knew you'd get bored after a-

T (shaky, out of breath): What the hell was that thing? Its face... I've never- WHAT IS HAPPENING?
The T checks the corner again and turns back.
T: Calm down, calm down. This has to be a prank. But... then... why does it feel so real? My heart... it's racing.
The camera pans out, displaying the size and scale of this place.
T (looking around): I've never seen anything like this in CS before. It has to just be a custom map, but... wait... I swear when we loaded in, it just said Cache.

T: If it was a custom map, it would've said something different, right? And, besides, if it was a map I hadn't played before the game would've made me download it. But I don't remember it doing that.

T: So then, is this place ACTUALLY real? Is it ACTUALLY part of the map?
