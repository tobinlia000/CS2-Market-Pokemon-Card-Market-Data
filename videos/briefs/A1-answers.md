# A1 — answers from the demo data (for the planning chat)

Everything below comes from the demo's recorded positions, view angles and events, checked against the real map
geometry. Times are demo time (m:ss.s). LL = Lightning Lemur, C = Caillou. Squares are floor A.
"Visible" means a clear line of sight to LL's body. Whether he's readable on screen in the map's darkness is
checked with test renders from C's view (see VISIBILITY TEST at the end).

## Headline findings (read first)
1. **LL is in plain sight more than the plan assumes.** He isn't hidden at W25/X25 or T14. He stands facing C,
   in C's line of sight and field of view, at:
   - 1:24–1:31: C faces north straight at him (about 700 u away).
   - 2:26–2:33: the same again.
   - 3:25–4:00: C stares north down the pillar hall at LL (about 1,300 u).
   So "the dark corridor he's staring into" (shots 7–8) *is* LL's position, and at 3:28–3:59 C is looking
   straight at LL.
   **Test render:** at 1:24–1:31 and 2:26–2:33 LL shows as a small silhouette in a lit doorway. At 3:25–4:00
   the hall is so dark he can't be seen at all.
2. **3:11–3:19: LL is right behind C,** 50–150 u away, after noclipping over. C faces north and never turns
   toward him (only for a moment at 3:11.0 and 3:17.5). It's a strong "it's right behind you" beat, but it
   breaks the HIDDEN plan for THE STOP. LL then noclips north through C's view (3:19.5–3:25) into the pillar hall.
3. **LL faces C almost the whole demo.** A camera behind C looking at LL will see LL's face. Face-safe angles are
   behind LL, or with C's head blocking him.
4. **No voice data is recorded** (checked). LL's "stepping noises" can only come from movement: running speed
   makes footsteps, while shift-walking (about 130 u/s) is silent in CS2. Most of LL's moves are silent walks or noclip.
5. **The corner scene:** C is **standing, not crouched** (crouched under 1 % of the time). The "runs to AG10/AG11"
   are **test drops into the pit** (it swallows him and pushes him back out). The final fall is fully visible.
