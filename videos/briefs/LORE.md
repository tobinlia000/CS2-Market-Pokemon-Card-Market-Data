# LORE & STORY BRIEFING — for the Cameraman
From the Creative Director (via the user, 2026-09-29). **This is the story bible: every shooting choice serves it.**
Camera decisions are the cameraman's; this says what the audience should feel, what they must never see, and what's
being set up for later.

## 1. The premise in one paragraph
A series of CS2 machinima videos (Part 1, 2, 3, plus earlier short-form videos). Two friends are making
smoke-lineup videos: the **CT** (the creator, "present-day me") and the **T** (his student). The T accidentally
phases through a wall on Cache into the **Skyboxes**, a liminal dimension above the maps, built from memories. The
twist, which the characters don't know: **the T is the CT's own past self**, a "still-life" who escaped the Skyboxes
years ago. The Skyboxes want him back, and **time itself**, in the form of an imposter copy of the CT, is trying to
put him there.

## 2. The world
**The Skyboxes** float literally in the sky above CS maps (clouds are visible outside windows). They're made of the
memories of everyone who played the game below: real-life places and old versions of maps, since CS was part of
growing up. What surrounds the rooms is unknown and never explained.

**Structure (a funnel that time uses to sort things, top to bottom):**
- **Top: the Backrooms and the Poolrooms.** Vast, bland, repeating spaces with almost no detail. General, shared
  nostalgia. This is where new or unsorted still-lifes land. The Backrooms are made of memories no one remembers
  anymore.
- **The Room of Doors.** A small, empty white room with about eight doors. Each door leads to a *category* of
  memories (grouped by feeling or time, not by person). Doors are locked or open by time's decision; the T is only
  ever allowed through one.
- **Specific memory rooms.** Detailed places: a Soviet school, a mall, a farm, a jungle temple, old maps. These sit
  lowest, closest to the players below. Each one has a still-life watching it.
- **The fake Mirage.** An amalgamation of maps pretending to be the present. It's the bridge back to reality.
- **The Hubble Deep Field room.** The imposter's favorite room (Part 2). It shows light from billions of years ago,
  a photo of old friends to time. It has no still-life, because time watches it himself.

**How memories work:** a room stays detailed and anchored while it's observed. When its still-life expires, the room
loses detail and drifts upward into the bland top levels.

**Ways in and out:** gaps in map geometry. Cache (freshly ported to CS2, glitchy) is how the T gets in. **Ancient**
is where the T originally fell out: Ancient was built from Aztec's assets, and its skybox is literally named after
Aztec in the game files. Aztec was removed from the game in 2017; that's when the T became a still-life.

## 3. The rules that affect the camera
**The core law is observation.** Being seen keeps you yourself. Being unseen makes you fade. Time only moves when it
isn't watched. **The camera counts as an observer in this world.** When the camera lingers on something, or chooses
to look, that's meaningful. Use that power deliberately.

**Still-lifes** (played by Lightning Lemur in the demos so far):
- Past selves in the T model. **They never face the camera. The audience must never read a still-life's front or
  face.** Face-safe angles: behind them, or with them out of frame. The only approved exceptions are tiny, dark,
  unreadable silhouettes in the distance.
- Their faces are **blank**. That reveal happens **only in the final shot of Part 1** (the jungle temple, blanked
  in editing). Nothing before it should show a face.
- Their one motivation is to be seen. The Backrooms still-life that seems to chase the T is actually a **guide**,
  trying to keep him observed and lead him down toward the exits. It's scary on first watch and sad on a rewatch.
  Frame it as menacing, but never cartoonishly evil.
- **Activity depends on depth:** top-level still-lifes are the most human (fast, frantic, mumbling, desperate). The
  deeper they're sorted, the calmer and stiller they get, until they're frozen. Progression across Part 1:
  Backrooms (fast, mumbling, even charging) → Poolrooms (drowning underwater, unseen) → School (staring down from a
  window, still) → Jungle temple (fully frozen).
- They **hide from the imposter by instinct**, and also from the CT (because he looks like the imposter). That's
  why rooms seem empty whenever the CT or the imposter is around.
- **The Poolrooms' still-lifes** fell into the water and are endlessly drowning, unobserved. Show them only from
  above the water line.

**Exit signs** are visible only to a present self (the CT), because a present mind can move between memories. The T
can't see them. Part 1 plants this quietly: a faint green glow or a blank exit sign the T walks right past.

**The imposter (time)** appears from Part 2 onward (and in the shorts). He's a copy of the CT's model. He **only
moves when no living, present being is watching him**. On screen, he should **never be seen moving while
observed**. The film language: we cut away, cut back, and he's closer.

**The chicken** is a real chicken that clipped into the Skyboxes and liked the barn. Living creatures move freely
between rooms and don't fade. It's comic relief and, eventually, the T's savior in Part 2.

**Things the camera must never show:**
- A still-life's front or face (except the approved distant silhouettes, and the final Part 1 reveal).
- The bottom of the Backrooms pit (it stays a mystery).
- The imposter moving while watched (Part 2+).
- Below the water line in the Poolrooms (and the water render breaks there anyway).

## 4. The characters
- **The T:** the past self. Removed with Aztec in 2017, frozen as a still-life for years, then slipped through into
  Ancient, where the CT saw him and being observed restored his face and personality (but not his memories). Scared
  of heights (he fell out of the sky). Doesn't know how to noclip (key plot point: the CT realizes this later).
  Jokey, nervous, talks to animals when alone. In the demos so far, **Caillou** plays the T.
- **The CT:** the present self and teacher. Doesn't know the T is his past self.
- **Lightning Lemur** plays the still-lifes.

## 5. The poem
An original poem exists in the world and is the story in miniature: the narrator is the present self, the sleeping
boy is the past self, the cloaked creature is time. The clock stops while the creature is watched; the creature
takes the boy by turning away. In Part 1 the poem is already pasted in the T's console, and later he half-sings its
opening in the corner: *"'Twas a late summer's night, only filled by a ticking, from the long-case clock, beside
which I was sitting, and I—"* (cut off right before the line about a faceless portrait).

## 6. The full story
**The shorts (already posted, the CT's side):** the T appears on Ancient and learns smokes. Then he vanishes. The CT
finds a video the T somehow posted from the Backrooms. The CT walks through the Cache wall onto the fake Mirage,
follows a running figure he thinks is the T, finds a still-life around a corner beside a door opened from the
inside, and follows it down into the Backrooms. He jumps into the pit, lands in the Poolrooms, meets the imposter
(walking backwards to keep it in view, asking the audience to watch it for him), jumps off the duck's ledge, and
reaches the Room of Doors.

**Part 1 (the T's side):** see §7. The T is trapped in the Backrooms, chased by a still-life, hides in a corner by
the pit, and falls in. Meanwhile the CT searches for him and eventually walks through the wall himself. The T passes
through the Poolrooms and into the Room of Doors, where only one door opens. A montage of rooms follows (school,
flashes, a farm where a chicken befriends him, a jungle temple). Part 1 ends with the camera staying behind after the
T falls, and revealing a still-life's blank face.

**Part 2:** the T and CT reunite in the Room of Doors. The CT sees exit signs the T can't. Their first room is the
Hubble room (time's favorite), which is how the imposter notices them. Doors open behind them: something is
following. In a mall, the CT teaches the T to press E on the chicken. Then the CT turns a corner into the imposter,
who steals his "present" and banishes him to the fake Mirage. The imposter now plays the CT (he can move while
watched, because he's carrying a living person's present) and leads the T astray. On an island, the chicken keeps
its distance from the "CT," the camera keeps getting distracted by scenery while the T falls behind, and doubled
footsteps (the real CT's echo, a half-beat late) unsettle the imposter. At a lamp post on a snowy night, the
imposter refuses to look at the T, and the T fades into a still-life, his face melting away. The camera starts to
leave, but the chicken runs past toward him, the camera follows, and the chicken's gaze restores him. The T asks the
camera to follow him. Part 2 ends with the reveal that **the T was the still-life on the fake Mirage who guided the
CT into the Backrooms in the shorts**.

**Part 3:** a time loop. The T sacrificed himself to lead the CT in; the banished CT resets to his entry point; the
loop repeats. It breaks when the camera observes the mall swap from a different angle, so the imposter can't take the
CT unseen. The CT reclaims his present, the imposter becomes freezable again, and the CT asks the audience to watch
the imposter while he and the T escape back to Ancient. The chicken goes home to its barn.

## 7. Part 1, scene by scene (story order, with intent)
Known mapping: **A1** = Backrooms (scenes 2 and 6, plus the end of 4), **A2** = Poolrooms (9), **C1** = School (11),
**D1** = Insertion2 (13), **G1** = de_lord, presumably the jungle temple (17).
1. **Cache, the stairs.** T and CT making a video; the T wonders about easter eggs, hears a noise, phases through the
   wall. Quick hook.
2. **Backrooms: trapped, chase, pillars (A1).** Disbelief turning into fear. He assumes it's a prank; noclip ("fly")
   fails; the poem is in his console. A distant silhouette (the source of the "No."). The reveal, the chase, the stop
   where he reasons that the place is real (while the still-life stands unseen right behind him), the footstep and
   whip pan, the charge, then counting pillars to stay oriented. Dread builds steadily; the Backrooms are wearing him
   down.
3. **Cache: the CT on the stairs, then a timelapse search.** Mild annoyance turning into worry. The CT assumes the T
   used noclip ("When did I even teach you how to do that?").
4. **Backrooms: 247, the pit, the corner.** He reaches the pit (child-like laughter from inside), backs into a
   corner, and walls the pit off with a smoke. The smoke fills the frame as the transition. *(The opening of this
   scene still needs to be recorded.)*
5. **Ancient: the CT redoing a lineup.** His smoke hits the old skybox; a glitch of light, and faint rain and thunder
   (Aztec's weather) under a clear sky. This is the exact spot where the T originally fell out.
6. **Backrooms: the creature returns (A1).** The T, worn down, lobs smokes and half-sings the poem ("I really hope
   that video posted"). The still-life approaches (creature-lead shots), appears behind him, and he backs away
   through his own smoke into the pit, swallowed by it. He's standing, not crouched, and walks backwards facing the
   still-life: both are canon now.
7. **Cache: the Buff ad, then the realization.** The CT admits his friend is still missing, realizes the T never knew
   how to noclip, and walks through the wall himself.
8. **The fake Mirage.** He doesn't notice anything's wrong; the camera does (odd details in a blurred foreground).
   "Where'd he go?" The camera sinks through the floor as the transition.
9. **Poolrooms (A2).** The T falls from the ceiling. Quiet, eerie calm; one shot from above a pool shows a still-life
   sinking far below (the T never notices). At the ledge, he jokes with the duck, admits he'd do anything to hear his
   friend's voice again, gets no music for his dramatic line, and jumps. Warmth and humor after the horror.
10. **Room of Doors.** Locked, locked, locked; the last door opens.
11. **School (C1).** The most liminal room: a real person's memory (the map maker recreated his old school; the
    girl's voice behind the locked door is a real recording of a classmate). A stopwatch counting up. Faint
    exit-sign plant. The still-life staring down from a window (still, calmer; he looks away). A strange gap in the
    grass. Get a lingering shot of the row of lamp poles: they foreshadow the lamp post in Part 2.
12. **Room of Doors, again.** "I was OUT." Same last door.
13–14. **Two flash maps (D1 Insertion2, plus one TBD).** A few seconds each: arrive, glimpse a still-life, clip out,
    white room. Fast rhythm.
15. **Farm.** A barn full of chickens standing perfectly frozen (unnerving, since CS chickens never stop moving). One
    turns its head. It follows him. (The frozen flock will be masked in editing.)
16. **Room of Doors, with the chicken.** "You came with me?"
17. **Jungle temple (G1).** Slow and quiet. It feels strangely familiar to him ("Why does this feel so familiar?"),
    though it's not his room. The chicken walks straight up to the still-life, unafraid. The T won't follow; he
    backs away, holding his gaze on it, and falls off the edge out of the map. **The camera does not follow him.** It
    stays, slowly moves around the still-life, and reveals its blank face. Music swells. End of Part 1. Emotional
    climax, not a scare: the whole theme is a choice between facing the past and running from it.

## 8. Tone and pacing guidance
- **Mostly static shots and slow zooms.** Specials are rare so they land.
- **Part 1's emotional arc:** confusion → dread → terror (Backrooms) → eerie calm and humor (Poolrooms) → melancholy
  wonder (School) → rhythm (montage) → charm (the chicken) → quiet sadness and awe (Jungle).
- **Pacing risk:** after the T falls into the pit, there's a long stretch with no direct threat (the ad, Mirage,
  Poolrooms, the Room of Doors, the School). Keep that stretch tight, and let small dread elements breathe (the
  drowning shot, the still-life in the window, the frozen flock).
- **Rewatch value matters.** Hidden details (a second still-life composited in the distance after the charge, the
  exit-sign plant, the lamp poles) reward attentive viewers. Subtle is better than obvious.
- **When in doubt about a story choice** (a beat that could reveal LL, reorder events, or change meaning), check with
  the Creative Director in the shared file. Everything else is the cameraman's call.

## Cameraman's standing rules (from the user; they override the above where they conflict)
1. **No noclip on screen:** footage where a player is noclipping is unusable (A1's LL "charge" is noclip from start
   to finish).
2. **LL's face/front is never readable:** the camera is clearly behind him (more than 120° from his facing) or he's
   out of frame. Silhouettes only when truly unreadable.
3. **Show the T (Caillou) moving;** standing only where the script has a beat, briefly. No long or redundant shots.
4. The map's own vignette is off (ze_backrooms: `r_csgo_postprocess_enable 0` plus a restored tone curve).
